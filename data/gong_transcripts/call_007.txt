Customer: Acme Technologies
Meeting: Engineering Planning Workflow Review
Date: 2026-08-01

Participants:
- Robert Miller, VP Engineering
- Aisha Khan, Engineering Manager
- Vikram Singh, Senior Software Engineer
- Priya Shah, Customer Success Manager

Priya:
I'd like to understand where the team spends time before developers actually
start writing code.

Robert:
Requirement refinement is one area. Product gives us requirements, but they
aren't always detailed enough for engineering to start immediately.

Aisha:
Our engineers spend quite a bit of time going through Jira tickets, reading
the comments, looking at related tickets, and figuring out exactly what the
requirement means.

Vikram:
Sometimes I'll spend 30 minutes just understanding the context of a ticket
before I can estimate it.

Priya:
What happens when the ticket isn't clear?

Vikram:
Usually I look through previous tickets or Slack conversations. If I still
can't figure it out, I schedule time with the product manager.

Aisha:
The problem is that this creates delays. Product thinks the requirement is
ready, but engineering discovers missing information during refinement.

Robert:
It's especially difficult for new engineers because they don't know the
history behind some of these systems.

Priya:
How often would you say engineers encounter this?

Aisha:
Pretty much every planning cycle. Some tickets are clear, but a meaningful
percentage need additional clarification.

Vikram:
I'd love something that could look at the ticket, related Jira issues, and
our existing documentation and summarize the context before I start working
on it.

Robert:
I'd also want it to identify missing information rather than just summarize
what's already there.

Aisha:
Exactly. If the system could say, "The acceptance criteria don't specify
what should happen in this scenario," that would be useful.

Priya:
Would AI-generated ticket analysis be something you'd pilot?

Robert:
Yes, provided engineers can verify the sources and it doesn't become another
system they have to maintain.

Vikram:
If it saves even 15 or 20 minutes per ticket, that would add up quickly.

Priya:
What would you measure?

Aisha:
Time spent in refinement, number of clarification cycles, and how often
tickets are returned to product for missing information.

Robert:
If those improve, I'd be interested in expanding it across engineering.
